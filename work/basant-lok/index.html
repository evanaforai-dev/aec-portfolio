reimagining community centres

basant lok, one of delhi's planned community centres in vasant vihar, redesigned around its metro gates, a city connector road and the greens the master plan requires.

role
urban designer, in a group of four with ejas, athul and sahiba
context
urban design studio, spa new delhi
year

the publication on behance ↗

in short

problem

a planned community centre cut through by a city connector road, with metro gates inside the site and greens the master plan makes mandatory.

what i did

read two precedents as dos and don'ts, mapped the neighbourhood and the site, and planned a mixed precinct around the three constraints that could not move.

result

a precinct of offices, housing and public space, drawn from neighbourhood scale down to floor plans.
