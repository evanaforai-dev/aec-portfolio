mobility design concept · kochi

vytilla junction

a concept for kochi's busiest junction: read from peak-hour traffic counts and signal capacity, then redrawn as loops that fold the mobility hub, the metro and the water metro into one system.

role
solo: analysis and design concept
context
mobility concept, kochi
year
2024
full project on behance ↗

in short

problem

vytilla junction stayed a bottleneck after the flyover was built, and at the morning peak every signalled arm ran over capacity.

what i did

read the junction from passenger car unit counts and signal timings, then redrew circulation as loops around two squares under the flyover, with the hub inside the junction rather than beside it.

result

a concept with no signals and no crossing paths, plus bus bays, pedestrian zones and parking for the first and last mile.

the problem

the mobility hub was built to fix vytilla. it cannot, while the junction outside it still stops every vehicle that reaches it.

circulation

vehicles loop around a square under the flyover, and a second loop takes traffic through the hub.

layers

eight junction details, then pedestrian zones, bus bays, greens and parking drawn as separate layers.

reflection

this is the junction outside my thesis site. the thesis designed the building and assumed the traffic; this project starts from the traffic.
