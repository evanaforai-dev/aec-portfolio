installation design · spa new delhi

into the tall grass

a kinetic canopy of grass-like strands, hung at human height, that rises to make a space around each person who walks under it. drawn from the fields and the walled city of amritsar.

role
designer, group project
context
semester project, spa new delhi
year
2021
full project on behance ↗

in short

problem

learning rarely leaves the four walls of a classroom, and public space rarely responds to the person standing in it.

what i did

a canopy of 1 m modules of sixteen strands each, lifted by motors when weight sensors in the floor detect someone, with each module drawn from a pattern in phulkari embroidery.

result

a private bubble that follows each visitor through the grid and closes again behind them.

how it responds

01

step onto the platform

a weight sensor sits at the centre of each 2 m square of floor

→↓
02

the modules overhead wake

the sensor signals the motors above it

→↓
03

the strands retract

a void opens around the person

→↓
04

the canopy swells

it is otherwise flat

→↓
05

step away

the canopy flattens and the strands fall again

the response follows each person's position on the grid, so the bubble moves with them.

the module

ripstop nylon strands on a motorised 1 m grid; each module is one motif of a phulkari.
