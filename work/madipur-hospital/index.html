hospital design studio · spa new delhi

madipur general hospital

a 600-bed general hospital in west delhi, with housing on the same site, planned around how patients, staff and services move, and checked against every rule that applies.

role
architect, in a group of three with athul raghunath and ejas muhammed
context
hospital design studio, spa new delhi
year
2021
full project on behance ↗

in short

problem

a hospital is the most regulated building most architects will draw: departments that must touch, flows that must never cross, and every bylaw at once.

what i did

zoned seventeen floors and two basements by level of care, then tested the scheme against coverage, setbacks, parking, fire access, stormwater and solar radiation.

result

a scheme where every department's position is argued by a rule or a flow, with every check done by hand.

the problem

in a hospital the plan is a set of adjacencies with a building around it. emergency beside diagnostics, critical care above emergency, and the services somewhere no visitor will ever see them.

checked by hand

coverage and setbacks

plot area, permissible ground coverage, the floor area split between hospital and housing, and the 15 m and 12 m setbacks, worked out in one table before any plan was drawn.

parking

2 car spaces per 100 m² of built-up area, met with a multi-level car park of about 510 spaces.

fire

a 6 m fire tender loop to the main road, fire stairs and cores within a 30 m radius of every point, and a pump house behind 120-minute fire walls.

stormwater

runoff from roofs, hardscape and softscape at a peak intensity of 35 mm an hour gives a load of about 651 m³ an hour, which sized eight rainwater harvesting pits.

sun

radiation studied in summer and winter. the long faces turn north and south, and the cores and ramp sit east and west as buffers.

reflection

every check on this page was done by hand, in a spreadsheet, on a scheme that kept changing under it, so a number checked on monday could be wrong by friday. that is the problem counterweight is built for.
