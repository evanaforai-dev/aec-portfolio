b.arch thesis · spa new delhi

gateway to kerala

phase ii of the vytilla mobility hub in kochi, india's first multimodal transit hub: a mixed-use development over a bus terminal, beside a metro station, on a river.

role
architect, solo thesis
context
b.arch thesis, school of planning and architecture, new delhi
year
2023
full thesis on behance ↗

in short

problem

vytilla is where kochi's buses, metro, water metro and national highway meet. the hub's second phase had to turn a place people pass through into one they stay in.

what i did

a podium of retail, culture and entertainment tied to the metro concourse, two towers of offices, a business centre and service apartments joined by a skywalk, and the river edge given back as public ground.

result

second best thesis at spa new delhi, 2023, and a charles correa gold medal finalist.

thesis

a transit hub is usually designed for the minute between two vehicles. this one asks what it would take for the same ground to work for the office-goer, the visitor, a family on a sunday, and the city seen from the flyover.

decisions the site made

01

no basement

why

the groundwater at vytilla sits too high to dig.

result

parking goes into a mechanical multi-level car park, levels 0 to 10, and every service gathers into one utility centre at grade.

02

the podium meets the metro

why

most people will arrive by train.

result

the podium connects at the metro concourse level, and all pedestrian circulation is lifted above the parking and service ground.

03

entertainment on top of retail

why

cinemas and amusement need long spans and make noise.

result

retail keeps only the first three floors, and the cineplex and indoor amusement sit above it, where their structure belongs.

04

two offices, one skywalk

why

government and private offices need separate addresses and drop-offs.

result

two towers, joined by a business centre and a skywalk at levels 20 to 24 that doubles as a vantage point.

05

the river as ground, not backdrop

why

activity is what keeps a river edge from neglect.

result

viewing decks step down to a waterfront for kayaking, boating and fishing.

reflection

this was the first time i had to hold a building, a junction, a river and a timetable in one drawing. the junction outside the site became a project of its own, and the city's transit app later became a product redesign.
