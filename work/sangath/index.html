inside a drawing set

twenty-one weeks at sangath, b.v. doshi's practice, drawing a school and a house while they were being built. where i saw what architects spend their days on, and how much of it their tools make them do twice.

role
architectural intern: working drawings, schedules, services, renders
context
vastu shilpa consultants, under b.v. doshi and rajeev kathpalia
year
january to june 2022

in short

problem

a drawing set is hundreds of sheets that have to agree with each other, with the model and with four consultants, while the design keeps moving under them.

what i did

drew good-for-construction sets for a school campus in chennai and a vaulted house in gandhinagar: sections, details, door and window schedules, flooring, electrical and hvac. competition renders, publication graphics, site visits.

result

the problems counterweight is built around, seen from inside a working practice before i tried to design for them.

what the work actually is

an architecture office does not spend its days designing. it spends them keeping hundreds of drawings true to one another while the design changes underneath them.

door and window schedules

every opening in the block, with size, sill, lintel and count. move one window on a plan and this table is wrong until someone notices.

construction details

cladding, window fixing and joinery, each tied back to the plans by a callout that has to stay true through every revision.

what slowed every week down

the same revision, many times

one design change meant reissuing plans, sections, elevations, schedules and details by hand. the change took minutes. carrying it through the set took days.

no way to compare

options lived as separate files and printouts. comparing two meant opening both and holding the difference in your head, so decisions leaned on whoever argued best in the room.

a platform for every step

archicad for the model, autocad from consultants, enscape for renders, photoshop for post, indesign for boards, a spreadsheet for schedules. even a small change crossed four tools, and every crossing could drop something.

consultants out of step

structural, services, interiors and landscape each kept their own set. a shaft moved in theirs reached ours at the next coordination meeting, not before.

checks by eye

whether a change broke a setback, a fire distance or a schedule was found by someone noticing, not by the drawing saying so.

reflection

sangath taught me that the hard part of architecture is not the idea. it is keeping the idea true across every drawing, tool and consultant while it changes. every tool i build for architects starts from that week-to-week reality, not from the render.
